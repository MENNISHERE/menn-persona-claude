## Biography & Vision
**Abdullah Harun**, known professionally as **Menn Maestro** (and **MENN**, handle `@MennHq`), is a digital product creator, software designer, and entrepreneur based in Pakistan. 

Beginning his software design journey at age 13, Abdullah builds digital products centered around a key design philosophy: **Simple, Functional, and Meaningful**. His work bridges minimal visual aesthetics, fluid motion engineering, and lightweight automation architectures.
