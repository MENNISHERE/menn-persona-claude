Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 4, 2026 | Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how MENN (operating under the identity of Abdullah Harun / Menn Maestro / MennHq, accessible via https://mennhq.vercel.app/) handles, respects, and protects user privacy.

1. Data Minimization & Collection Philosophy

We believe digital software and showcase platforms should respect user privacy by default. We do not harvest, store, rent, or sell personal browsing information. We do not require visitors to register an account or provide personal credentials simply to explore our showcases and documentation.

2. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

Our website is cookie-free. We do not place third-party advertising cookies or behavioral tracking pixels on your device. We utilize standard client-side browser local storage (localStorage) exclusively to store your personal interface preferences (such as theme and motion settings) locally on your device without transmitting this information to external servers.

3. Server Logs & Edge Hosting Infrastructure

When you visit https://mennhq.vercel.app/, our hosting infrastructure provider (Vercel Inc.) may collect standard, anonymized server access telemetry (such as IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, and request timestamps) solely for operational reliability, DDoS mitigation, and global edge network routing.

4. Outbound Links & External Networks

Our website features outbound connections to verified social platforms (X, GitHub, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Behance) and documentation endpoints. MENN does not control the data handling practices of these external providers. We encourage visitors to review the privacy statements of any third-party websites they navigate to.

5. AI Agent & Crawler Policy

We welcome legitimate search engine crawlers, autonomous AI agents, and LLM indexers to read our machine-readable endpoints (such as /llms.txt, /llms-full.txt, /openapi.json, and /.well-known/mcp). Automated scraping that degrades server availability or attempts unauthorized penetration is strictly prohibited.
