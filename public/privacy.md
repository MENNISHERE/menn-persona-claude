## 1. Data Minimization
MENN operates on a privacy-first, zero-tracking policy. We do not harvest, store, rent, or monetize personal information. 

## 2. Cookies & Local Storage
- **Cookies:** This platform does not deploy third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
- **Local Storage:** Client-side `localStorage` is used solely to maintain user UI preferences on the user's local machine.

## 3. Server Logs
Standard anonymized telemetry (IP, user agent, request timestamp) is processed by the hosting edge provider (Vercel) exclusively for DDoS mitigation and performance routing.

## 4. Third-Party Links
External links to GitHub, X, Instagram, LinkedIn, and YouTube are subject to the respective privacy policies of those independent providers.
